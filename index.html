The problem

It’s not the big stuff keeping you up

Some of what’s running in your head is big. Work. Money. Whether you’re doing right by the people you love.

But most of it is small. The email you owe. The receipt. The permission slip. The thing you said you’d look into. The plumber, who is now overdue.

Each small thing is light. Together they drown out everything else.

You don’t need a better memory. You need somewhere to put things.

The system

One inbox. Three folders.
A few minutes a day.

It runs on the notes app already on your phone. Here’s the whole thing:

All day
Capture

Something lands in your head? Put it in the inbox. Ten seconds.

Every evening
Empty

Five minutes. Each item goes to Active, Reference, Archive, or the trash.

Sunday
Look

Fifteen minutes. Clear out what’s done. Notice what’s stuck.

No tags. No dashboards. No new app to learn.

Why it works

What a sleep lab found

~9 min
faster to fall asleep

In a 2018 Baylor University sleep-lab study, people who spent five minutes before bed writing down what was still undone fell asleep faster than people who wrote about what they’d finished. The more specific the list, the faster they fell asleep.

One small study, of college students, over one night. It proves nothing about you. But the idea is simple: once it’s written down somewhere you trust, your mind can stop rehearsing it.
